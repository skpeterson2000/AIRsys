Symbols (A)

Check

    Open a drawing

    Keys

    Afind a symbol to place
    Wdraw a line; click to bend, double-click or click a port to finish
    /while drawing, bend the other way first
    Backspacewhile drawing, take back the last bend
    L / Tnet label / note
    R / Xrotate / mirror the selection, or the part being placed
    Mmove the selection with the mouse; drag works too, and lines stay attached
    Eedit the selected part's number, value and fields
    Deldelete
    Ctrl+Dduplicate
    Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Yundo / redo
    Ctrl+Ssave
    Homefit the sheet in the window
    Wheelzoom about the cursor; drag with the middle button, or Space and drag, to pan
    Escstop what you are doing, back to select

    Clicking an open port (the small circle at the end of a port) starts a line from it.
